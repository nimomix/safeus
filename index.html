When uncertainty hits, hold on to one thing.

A missile alert. An earthquake. A late-night ride home. A quiet day that suddenly stops feeling quiet.

In those moments you don't want to coordinate, draft a message, or call ten people back. You want to know — fast — that the people you love are OK, and you want them to know the same about you.

Slide once to mark yourself unsafe — your contacts get your location at that moment, a clear way to call you, and a tap to navigate straight there.

Always know who's OK.

Your contacts' status is always there — quietly, without asking. Green check, marked safe just now. The whole family, all OK.

No group chats to scroll. No "did you see this?" No waiting for a reply that may never come.

Group your contacts into Family, Friends, or Work. Get notified when everyone in a group is safe. Send a gentle nudge to anyone you haven't heard from.

How it works

Tap "I'm Safe"

One tap broadcasts your status to everyone you've added.

See who's OK

Live status updates from the people who matter — and a green check when a whole group is safe.

Nudge, gently

Haven't heard from someone? A quiet ping that asks "are you OK?" without making it a thing.

Calm, by day or night.

Designed for the moments you reach for your phone in a hurry — at 2 a.m., on the train, in a stairwell, in the dark.

Built quietly, on purpose.

Safeus exists to give peace of mind, not to harvest it. We never sell your data, and we never upload your address book — only the phone numbers Safeus needs to find people you already know on the app.
